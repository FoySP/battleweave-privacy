Battleweave: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026. This policy covers the Battleweave game for Android (package com.battleweave.game). The developer is Steve Foyston, contactable at stevefoyston@gmail.com.

In short: your game is stored on your phone. If you open the online Arena, the game sends a random code, your player name and your team to our server so you can fight other players and appear on the leaderboard. There are no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Payments are handled by Google Play. You can delete your online data from the game's Settings at any time.

What stays on your phone

Your heroes, gear, progress, settings and the record of what you have bought are saved in the game's own storage on your phone. Android may back this up with your own Google account backup, if you have that switched on.

What is sent to our server

Only when you use the online Arena, the leaderboard or change your player name (the game needs an internet connection for these):

DataWhy
A random code made on your phone when you first play. The server stores only a one-way hash of it, not the code itself.So the same phone gets the same arena profile again. It is not linked to your name, email or Google account.
Your player name (letters, digits and a few symbols, up to 16 characters; offensive names are replaced with “Weaver”).Shown to other players on the leaderboard and as your arena name.
Your arena team: which heroes, their levels, stars, gear and cosmetic bonuses.Other players fight it as your defence team.
Your arena rating, the fights you were offered and the moves you made in them.To check that results are fair and to work out ratings and the leaderboard.
Dates the profile was created and last used.Running and securing the service.

The server does not collect your name (real name), email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone or advertising ID. Your IP address is not stored with your profile, though the server's ordinary technical logs may briefly contain it. Connections to the server are encrypted (HTTPS). The server is rented from a hosting company; it processes this data for us and does not use it for anything else.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The game records on your phone which products you have bought so that you keep them; this record is not sent to our server.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone for their own purposes.

Other players

Other players can see your player name, your arena rating and your arena team. They cannot see anything else about you, and there is no chat.

Keeping and deleting data

Online data is kept while your profile is in use. To delete it, open Settings → Delete my online data in the game. This removes your name, arena team, rating entry, sign-in link and sessions from the server straight away; only a blank record of the profile’s id and dates remains. You can also ask by email (see the deletion page). Server backups are overwritten within 14 days. Deleting your online data does not affect the game saved on your phone; uninstalling the game removes that.

Children

Battleweave is not directed at children under 13, and the game contains optional purchases of random items. Please do not use it if you are under 13. Younger players should have a parent’s permission to buy anything.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office). Email stevefoyston@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes, and the game will say so if the change is important.
