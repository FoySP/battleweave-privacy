Delete your Battleweave online data

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Battleweave and tap the cog (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Tap Delete my online data, then Delete.

This removes your player name, arena team, leaderboard entry, sign-in link and sessions from our server straight away.

By email

If you no longer have the game installed, email stevefoyston@gmail.com from any address with the subject “Delete my Battleweave data” and your player name, and we will remove the matching profile within 30 days. Because the game has no accounts or email addresses, we may ask for your arena rating and team to be sure it is yours.

What is kept

A blank record of the profile’s id and dates, and server backups for up to 14 days. Your game on your phone is not touched; uninstalling the game removes it.
